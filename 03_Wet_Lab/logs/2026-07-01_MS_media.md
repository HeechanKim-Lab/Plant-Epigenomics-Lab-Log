# 1/2 MS Media (2026-07-01)

## Protocol & Notes

### Reference Protocol: 1/2 MS Media Formulation

| Component | Amount (per 1 L) | Target Specification |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Murashige & Skoog (MS) Medium | 2.15 g | 1/2 Strength Basal Medium |
| Sucrose | 10 g | Carbon Source |
| MES | 0.5 g | Buffering Agent |
| Plant Agar | 7 g | Solidifying Agent |
| pH | — | 5.8 |

#### Step-by-Step Procedure

* **1)** Dissolve 2.15 g of MS medium, 10 g of sucrose, and 0.5 g of MES in Distilled Water (D.W.) to a volume close to 1 L.
* **2)** Adjust the pH of the solution to 5.8 using 1 M KOH or 1 M HCl.
* **3)** Bring the final volume exactly to 1 L with D.W.
* **4)** Split the adjusted liquid medium equally into two containers (500 mL per container).
* **5)** Add 3.5 g of plant agar to each 500 mL container (totaling 7 g for the 1 L batch).
* **6)** Autoclave at 121°C for 15 minutes.

## Bench Execution Log & Deviations

1. **Glassware Preparation and Rinsing**
   * Prepared the necessary glassware for the 1 L media preparation batch:
     * One 1 L beaker (for initial dissolving)

     * One 1 L graduated cylinder
     
     * One 500 mL graduated cylinder
    
     * Two 1 L Erlenmeyer (triangular) flasks (for autoclaving and storage)
   * Thoroughly rinsed all selected glassware with Distilled Water (D.W.) prior to use to prevent any environmental or chemical contamination.

2. **Reagent Weighing and Initial Solubilization**
   * Dispensed approximately 800 mL of Distilled Water (D.W.) into the 1 L beaker.
   * Inserted a clean magnetic stir bar into the beaker, placed the assembly onto the magnetic stir plate, and switched the stirrer on to initiate consistent agitation.
   * Powered on the electronic balance, positioned a sheet of weighing paper on the pan, and tared the balance to zero.
   * Decontaminated the laboratory spoon by thoroughly wiping it with a 70% Ethanol swab prior to handling the reagents.
   * Accurately weighed out the required amounts of Murashige & Skoog (MS) Basal Medium (2.15 g), Sucrose (10 g), and MES Buffer (0.5 g) sequentially, and transferred them directly into the stirring D.W. to allow complete dissolution.

3. **Solidifying Agent Dispensing and Vessel Sealing**
   * Accurately weighed two separate portions of **3.5 g of Plant Agar** using the electronic balance.
   * Transferred 3.5 g of the agar directly into each of the two 1 L Erlenmeyer flasks.
   * Labeled both flasks clearly.
   * Covered the mouth of each flask securely with aluminum foil to close them.

4. **pH Meter Calibration and Media pH Adjustment**
   * Setup the pH meter and performed a standard two-point calibration using pH 4.0 and pH 7.0 reference calibration solutions to ensure measurement accuracy.
   * Placed the pH electrode into the stirring beaker containing the dissolved MS, sucrose, and MES mixture.
   * Added 1 M KOH dropwise under continuous stirring until the solution reached an exact pH of 5.8.

5. **Final Volumetric Adjustment and Media Aliquoting**
   * Transferred the pH-adjusted solution from the 1 L beaker into a 1 L graduated cylinder.
   * Brought the final volume exactly to 1 L by adding Distilled Water (D.W.).
   * Used a 500 mL graduated cylinder to measure and dispense exactly 500 mL of the liquid medium into each of the two 1 L Erlenmeyer flasks containing the pre-weighed plant agar, ensuring an equal volumetric split of the 1 L batch.

6. **Autoclaving and Media Sterilization**
   * Placed the two foil-sealed 1 L Erlenmeyer flasks containing the 1/2 MS media and plant agar mixture into the autoclave chamber.
   * Operated the autoclave using the standard liquid sterilization cycle set at **121°C for 15 minutes**.
   * Following the complete depressurization of the chamber, safely retrieved the flasks and transferred them to a clean bench environment for subsequent cooling and pouring.
